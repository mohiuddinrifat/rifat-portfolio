About Me

A bit about my studies and interests

I’m Md. Mohiuddin Rifat, a fourth-year student in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Jamalpur Science & Technology University. Most of my time is spent learning about engineering concepts and figuring out how they work in practice.

I’m especially interested in electrical power systems, electronics, electrical machines, communication systems, and circuit design. These subjects have given me plenty to explore, both in class and during laboratory work.

Outside my core EEE subjects, I’m learning programming and web development. I try to build my skills step by step, learn from mistakes, and take on projects that help me connect theory with practical work. I’m looking forward to gaining more experience as I continue my journey in engineering.
