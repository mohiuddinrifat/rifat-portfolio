My Projects

A selection of academic and personal projects I've worked on

🔐

Smart Attendance Using Fingerprint

Developed a smart attendance system using fingerprint recognition to make the attendance process easier, faster, and more reliable. The system uses biometric technology to identify individuals and record their attendance automatically, reducing the need for manual entry and minimizing errors.

This project gave me practical experience in working with fingerprint sensors, hardware interfacing, and embedded system development. It also helped me understand how automation can be used to solve everyday problems.

Category: Embedded Systems
⚡

DC-DC Buck Converter

Designed and developed a DC-DC Buck Converter to step down a 12 volt DC voltage to a 3 volt regulated DC output. The project focuses on the working principle of switching converters and efficient power conversion using electronic components.

Through this project, I gained practical knowledge of power electronics, PWM control, circuit design, and voltage regulation. It also helped me understand how DC-DC converters are used in power supplies and electronic devices.

Category: Power Electronics | Circuit Design
☀️

Water Purifier Using Sunlight

Developed a water purification system that uses sunlight as a natural source of energy to help make water safer for everyday use. The project explores the use of solar energy for water purification, offering an affordable and environmentally friendly approach, especially for areas with limited access to clean drinking water.

Through this project, I gained practical knowledge of renewable energy, solar energy applications, and sustainable engineering solutions. It also helped me understand how engineering can contribute to solving real-world problems.

Category: Renewable Energy | Sustainable Engineering
